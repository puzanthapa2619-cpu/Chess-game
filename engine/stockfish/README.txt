Drop your Stockfish binary here to enable strong AI play.

Download from: https://stockfishchess.org/download/

- Linux/macOS: rename executable to `stockfish` and `chmod +x stockfish`
- Windows    : name it `stockfish.exe`

The app auto-detects Stockfish in this folder or on your PATH. Without it,
a built-in Python engine is used instead (still fully playable).
